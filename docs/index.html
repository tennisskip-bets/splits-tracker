Line moves are measured from the first snapshot, not the true opening line. Flags only use snapshots before tip-off. Majority flips show as notes, not flags. A reverse move needs a 1-point spread move (1.5 on totals) toward one side while 60%+ of bets or money sit on the other. These are prompts to look closer, not betting advice, and reverse movement is not a proven edge.
